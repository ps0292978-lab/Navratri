Aaj Navratri ka pehla din hai, aur is special din par main bas tumhare liye dil se ek pyari si wish karna chahta hoon. Maa Durga tumhari life ko hamesha khushiyon se bhar de, tumhare chehre ki smile kabhi kam na ho, aur tumhari life mein jitni bhi pareshaniyan aayein, Maa tumhe un sabse ladne ki strength de.

Jaise Navratri ki roshni har taraf positivity lekar aati hai, waise hi tumhari smile bhi hamesha tumhare aas-paas khushiyan failati rahe. ❤️

Mujhe pata hai tum chhoti-chhoti baaton ko lekar bahut sochte ho aur tension lete ho. Aur I know, kabhi-kabhi main usmein aag mein ghee daalne jaisa kaam karta hoon. Lekin main kya karun yaar, mujhse galtiyan ho jaati hain, par main jaanbujhkar nahi karta.

Main tumse rudely baat nahi karta. Main shayad aisa isliye ho gaya hoon kyunki meri life mein log hamesha mujhe chhodkar chale jaate hain. Lekin tumhare liye meri feelings bilkul jhoothi nahi hain. Maine tumhe apni life ka ek bahut important person maana hai. ❤️

Mujhe nahi pata yaar, main tumse itna pyaar kyun karta hoon. Maine kabhi socha bhi nahi tha ki mujhe dobara kabhi pyaar hoga, lekin jab se maine tumhe dekha hai aur tumse baat karna shuru kiya hai, meri feelings har din aur badhti ja rahi hain.

Jab tum mujhe kehte ho ki tum mujhe sirf ek friend ki tarah dekhte ho, toh mujhe bahut bura lagta hai. Aur jab tum gusse mein mujhe block kar dete ho, toh aisa lagta hai jaise kisi ne mere dil mein chaku maar diya ho.

Phir bhi main 1% hope lekar chalta hoon ki shayad kabhi tum meri feelings samajh paoge. Maine tumse door hone ki koshish bhi ki, lekin mujhse nahi ho paata. I don't know ki ise pyaar kahun ya pagalpan, but one thing I know is that I love you, aur mere dil mein tumhare liye jo feelings hain, woh sachchi hain.

Sorry yaar, itna kuch bol diya. Tum khush raho, tumhari khushi mein hi meri khushi hai.

HAPPY NAVRATRI 🌸
